# Physical constants

## Speed of light

The speed of light in vacuum is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second by the
definition of the metre in the International System of Units (SI).

This exact value applies in vacuum. Light travels more slowly through materials
such as air, water, and glass.

Source: Bureau International des Poids et Mesures, *The International System of
Units (SI Brochure)*, ninth edition.
